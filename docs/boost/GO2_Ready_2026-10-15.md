## Boost answered all five of Tim's questions
| Tim's question | Boost, 15 Oct |

Boost also corrected the garbled sentence: "disabling Search Personalization does not automatically disable Product
Performance Ranking. The two features are configured independently, so Product Performance
Ranking remains active unless it is explicitly disabled." That confirms stopping was right.

## Recommend Option A, not B
Under Option A we can re-enable Search Personalization ourselves the moment anything looks
wrong, without waiting for Boost. Under Option B both halves of the rollback sit with the
vendor. Given Tim's instruction to restore early for a material regression, keeping half the
rollback in our own hands is worth the slightly messier switch.

**The constraint Tim needs to know:** Product Performance Ranking cannot be re-enabled by us
under either option. The restore depends on Boost being available. So the window must be one
where Boost is staffed for the restore, not just for the disable.

## What GO 2 will and will not fix
Boost's read of our evidence, which matches ours:
- **Items 1, 4 and 5** (45 kettlebell at rank 8, the Rubber Hex parent missing from its own
  query, the FFS Silver pulley at rank 4) are "being reranked by Search Personalization and
  Product Performance Ranking". These are exactly what GO 2 tests.
- **Item 2**, bumper plate 45-pound returning nine sets, is a unit-vocabulary problem. Boost
  treat lb, lbs and pound as separate keywords and recommend Manual Synonyms lb to lbs and
  lb to pound. GO 2 will not fix this.
- **Item 3**, assault bike returning zero, is **not a search defect at all**. See below.

## "assault bike" is a catalog vocabulary gap, not a ranking failure
Boost searched our Shopify admin independently and found no product containing "assault".
That matches our own check: zero active products match "assault" on title, SKU, vendor, type
or tags.

Verified in the Shopify Admin API on 15 Oct, we sell exactly two products of that kind:

| Product | ID |
|---|---|
| French Fitness FF-ABM40 Air Bike Machine w/Air Resistance (New) | 9878922264892 |
| French Fitness FF-AFB Air Fan Bike (New) | 9878925607228 |

"Assault bike" is a genericised brand name, from Assault Fitness, that shoppers use for air
or fan bikes generally. Customers typing it want these two products. Nothing in the catalog
carries the word, so no ranking change can surface them.

The fix is a synonym or a redirect mapping assault bike to air bike and fan bike, which is a
separate change needing its own approval. It should be recorded as a vocabulary gap and taken
out of the GO 2 success criteria, otherwise it will read as an unchanged failure when it was
never a ranking problem.

Related: there is an existing internal thread titled "Search field fails to return French
Fitness FF-AFB Air Fan Bike when using full keyword phrase", so this product already has a
known search history worth folding in.
