- The field holding product class/category, plus Product Type: Medium

Boost also listed the 10 searchable fields and their data sources: Title (title), Barcode

They asked us to identify which field actually holds our product class.

## 4. Answer: it is Product Category, not Product Type
Verified against the Shopify Admin API on 12 Oct.

**Product Type is unusable**, unchanged from the earlier finding: of 3,755 active products,
3,735 carry the single value "Product Index", 17 are "Product (Hidden)", 3 are blank.

**Product Category is populated with real equipment class.** Sampled 100 active products
across two pages: 95 carry a specific taxonomy category, 5 are "Uncategorized". Values seen
include Elliptical Trainers, Treadmills, Smith Machines, Stair Climbers & Steppers, Exercise
Benches, Weight Lifting Machines with Pulleys, Dumbbells, Free Weight Storage Racks,
Recumbent Exercise Bikes, Upright Exercise Bikes, Floor Protection Mats and Exercise Machine
& Equipment Sets.

This is a sample, not a census. The exact Uncategorized count should be read from Shopify
admin, Products, filtered by Category is Uncategorized, before anything is weighted on it.

Tags were considered and rejected as the class field. They are heavily overloaded: one
product in the sample carried 23 tags mixing class, brand, product line and the URL handle.
Raising Tags to Medium would import that noise into ranking.

**So Boost's revised recommendation is workable if "the field containing product class" is
read as Product Category.** Product Type should stay where it is or go to Low, since it
carries no signal.
