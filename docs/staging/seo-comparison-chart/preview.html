How to choose

Dedicated cable station

FFB/FFS DAP and DAP50 use conventional dual-adjustable-pulley layouts. Telluride prioritizes open-arm crossover geometry and a larger workout envelope.

Rack plus cables, no Smith

The wall-mounted 1:1 and 2:1 systems require structural anchoring. SRFT8 is the freestanding rack, cable, landmine, and storage option.

Rack, cables, and Smith

FSR90 uses a sourced 45 lb non-counterbalanced Smith bar. FSR110 uses a counterbalanced Smith with a much lower stated starting resistance.

Frequently asked questions

Functional trainer comparison FAQs

Are the FFB Black and FFS Silver dual adjustable pulleys different machines?

The current product source states that the FFS Silver is the same machine as the FFB Black except for color. Their sourced dimensions, product weight, standard stacks, optional stacks, and 2:1 ratio match, so this guide treats them as one color family.

Which selected French Fitness functional trainer is shortest?

The DAP50 is the shortest selected system at 82.6 inches high. The SRFT8 is next at 86.57 inches. Allow added clearance for assembly, pull-up use, ceiling obstructions, and exercise movement.

What is the practical difference between a 1:1 and 2:1 pulley ratio?

On the sourced wall-mounted models, 1:1 provides direct selected-stack resistance. The 2:1 version provides approximately half the selected stack weight at the handle while allowing longer cable travel and smaller effective increments.

Which selected systems include a Smith machine?

The FSR90 and FSR110 include Smith machines. The FSR90 uses a sourced 45-pound non-counterbalanced Smith bar. The FSR110 uses a counterbalanced Smith with sourced starting resistance of approximately 5.5 pounds per side.

Which selected systems combine a rack and cables without a Smith machine?

The SRFT8 combines a squat rack, dual cable columns, pull-up station, landmine, and storage without a Smith carriage. The two wall-mounted models also combine rack and cable functions without a Smith machine, but require structural wall anchoring.

How should I compare machine dimensions with room size?

Treat listed dimensions as the equipment footprint, not the complete workout area. Allow extra room for cable travel, benches, barbells, spotter arms, rotating arms, pull-ups, loading, access, and safe circulation. Telluride separately states folded dimensions and a maximum workout footprint.
