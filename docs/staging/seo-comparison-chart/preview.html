Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a 1:1 and 2:1 pulley ratio?

A 1:1 system generally delivers resistance close to the selected stack amount at the handle. A 2:1 system generally delivers about half the selected stack amount while providing more cable travel and smaller effective weight increments.

Which models are dedicated cable stations?

The FFB/FFS DAP family, DAP50 and Telluride are dedicated cable systems without an integrated barbell rack or Smith carriage.

Which models include a squat rack but no Smith machine?

The wall-mounted 1:1 and 2:1 systems and the SRFT8 combine rack training with dual adjustable pulleys without adding a Smith carriage.

Which models include a Smith machine?

The FSR90 and FSR110 integrate a Smith system. The FSR90 uses a non-counterbalanced 45 lb Smith bar, while the FSR110 uses a counterbalanced Smith system with a much lower stated starting resistance.

Does the smallest machine always require the least room?

No. Equipment dimensions do not include all user movement, bench, plate-loading, cable-travel or access clearance. The Telluride also has separate maximum-workout and folded dimensions.
