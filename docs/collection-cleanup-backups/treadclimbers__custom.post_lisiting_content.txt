<h2>Why Choose a TreadClimber?</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Efficient calorie burn:</strong> The hybrid stepping + walking path delivers more output at lower speeds.</li>
    <li><strong>Low-impact training:</strong> Joint-friendly motion is ideal for long sessions and rehab-minded users.</li>
    <li><strong>Compact footprint:</strong> Fits where separate stepper + treadmill + elliptical won’t.</li>
    <li><strong>Commercial-grade options:</strong> Rugged alternatives are available (see below).</li>
  </ul>

<h2>FAQs About Bowflex TreadClimbers</h2>

  <h3>Are Bowflex TreadClimbers discontinued?</h3>
  <p>Yes. All models that we sell are legacy, but remanufactured units are available via Fitness Superstore with warranty options.</p>

  <h3>Which models burn the most calories?</h3>
  <p>Higher-spec units (TC5300, TC5500, TC6000, TC20) support faster speeds and advanced programs that help sustain tougher sessions.</p>

  <h3>Do you offer delivery and installation?</h3>
  <p>Yes. We ship nationwide and offer white-glove installation (recommended for stairs/tight spaces).</p>

  <h3>What ceiling height do I need?</h3>
  <p>Because the motion raises user height, measure clearance above your tallest user. Contact us for model-specific guidance.</p>

  <h3>Do these units include a warranty?</h3>
  <p>Yes—coverage is shown on each product page. Extended options are available for additional peace of mind.</p>
