# Google feed offer ID — the rule, and the 114-row correction

## The rule

**Google offer id = variant SKU. Never the numeric Shopify product ID, never
flipped once set.** Per Tim, Sept 7 2026, permanent.

An identity flip resets that offer's Google performance history, so an id is
changed only to correct a defect, never as cleanup.

Feeds are generated by **Multifeeds** (`multiple-google-shopping-feed`, by
WoolyTech), configured in the app's admin. The mapping is not in this repo.

### Two standing exceptions

1. **Three SKUs are themselves numeric** — `931`, `933`, `935` (Precor 9.31 /
   9.33 / 9.35 Premium Series treadmills, products `9878495265084`,
   `9878495887676`, `9878496379196`). They already satisfy the rule. Any
   "reject numeric offer id" validation must exempt them or it will flag three
   correct offers forever.

2. **The Local Inventory Ads feed keys 45 offers on
   `<product ID>-<variant ID>`**, all on product `10247596147004`. That scheme
   is correct and is not covered by this rule — see
   `docs/local-inventory-feed.md`.

## The 114-row correction — validation, 2026-09-08

`docs/woolytech_id_corrections_114_VALIDATED.csv` is Tim's correction file with
verification columns added. Every row was checked against live Shopify.

| Check | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Rows | 114 (13 `googleshoppingfrenchfitness`, 101 `googleshoppingfs`) |
| `correct_sku_id` exists on the named product | 114 / 114 |
| Product ACTIVE | 114 / 114 |
| Target SKUs distinct within the file | 114 / 114 |
| Collision rows | 14, matching Tim's count |
| Collision groups fully enumerated | 3 / 3, no variant missing |

The file is sound. Three findings qualify how it is applied.

### 1. Three rows are no-ops

`931`, `933`, `935` have `current_numeric_id` equal to `correct_sku_id`. They
were swept in by a "looks numeric" scan but are already correct. **111 rows are
real changes, not 114.** Rewriting the other three would reset their
performance history for no gain.

### 2. The collision fix restores 11 dropped offers

| Product | Variants | Offers today | Offers after |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `10269254254908` French Fitness Monster Universal Storage System | 10 | 1 | 10 |
| `9878898540860` Star Trac 8 Series Rear Drive Elliptical 16" | 2 | 1 | 2 |
| `9878900179260` Star Trac 8 Series TR Treadmill 20" | 2 | 1 | 2 |

103 distinct offer IDs today become 114. The French Fitness storage system is
the bulk of it — 9 of its 10 variants are dropped by Google right now.

### 3. Two target SKUs are not unique catalog-wide

| SKU | Also on | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ST-8RDE-16-ATSC-OOB` | `10456972788028` | DRAFT |
| `ST-8TR-20-ATSC-OOB` | `10456978325820` | DRAFT |

Both duplicates are hidden draft shadows of the OOB variants, so neither
reaches a feed today and neither blocks the correction. But keying on SKU means
**publishing either draft recreates the same collision the correction just
fixed**, under a SKU instead of a numeric ID. Before publishing an OOB product,
check that its SKU is not already live on another product.

## Verification after the change

In Merchant Center, confirm:

- `ST-8TR-20-ATSC-OOB` exists as its own offer.
- Offer count rises by 11, not by 114 — the other 103 are re-keys of offers
  that already existed.
- Regenerate `snippets/local-inventory-offer-allowlist.liquid` from fresh
  exports afterward; the primary `id` column is its source.
