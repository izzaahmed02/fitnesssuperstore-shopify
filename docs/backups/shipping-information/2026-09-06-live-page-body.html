At Fitness Superstore, we’re more than just a leading supplier of gym equipment—we’re a fast-growing, people-powered organization that values innovation, commitment, and excellence. With over a decade of success and thousands of satisfied customers across the globe, we continue to expand our operations and are looking for passionate individuals to grow with us.

Who We Are

Founded in 2010, Fitness Superstore has grown into one of the most trusted sources for new and remanufactured gym equipment in the United States. Our headquarters and warehouse operations are based in Benicia, California, where we house our factory, assembly line, and offices. With a workforce of 90 employees, we pride ourselves on building a company culture that fosters collaboration, creativity, and continuous growth.

Why Join Us?

At the heart of our company is a belief in opportunity– not just for our customers, but also for our team members. Whether you’re a seasoned professional or just beginning your journey, Fitness Superstore offers an environment where your contributions are recognized, your growth is supported, and your success is shared.

We offer:

  • Competitive compensation

  • Opportunities for professional development

  • A supportive, team-oriented culture

  • Flexible remote and hybrid work arrangements (depending on role)

  • Direct impact on the growth of a national brand

Departments and Roles

As we expand, we are actively hiring in a range of departments including:

  • Operations – Roles in warehousing, logistics, assembly, and quality control help ensure our equipment gets to customers efficiently and in perfect condition.

  • Marketing – Content creators, digital strategists, and multimedia artists to help us build our brand and reach new audiences.

  • Administrative – From customer service to human resources, our administrative team keeps our business running smoothly every day.

We're always on the lookout for individuals with energy, adaptability, and a drive to make things better. If you enjoy problem-solving, value customer satisfaction, and thrive in a team setting, there's likely a place for you here.

How to Apply

Interested in joining us? We’d love to hear from you. To apply, please send your resume and a brief introduction to info@fitnesssuperstore.com. Be sure to include the role you're interested in and any relevant experience. Our team will review your application and contact you if your skills align with our current openings.

If you're curious about available positions or would like more information, feel free to reach out to the same email.
